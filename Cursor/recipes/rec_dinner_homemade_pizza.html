Homemade Pizza
Ingredients:
  • Dough
  • Mozzarella
  • Tomatoes/Pasta Sauce
  • Black Olives
  • Pepperoni
  • Salt
  • Semolina Flour
Steps:
  1. Semolina flour and salt on a tray with olive oil
  2. Knead dough into a circle of desired size
  3. Put sauce, mozzarella, pepperoni, and black olives on top
  4. Cook pizza in oven at 400F for 20 minutes
